1. Sales and revenue analytics
(High priority)

These charts are especially useful for restaurant owners and managers.

1. Revenue Trend – Area Chart

Search: Interactive gradient area chart
- Daily, weekly and monthly revenue.
- Compare current period with previous period.
- Hover to see exact revenue.
- Filter by outlet.
Data: Sales transactions / Petpooja integration.
2. Revenue Comparison – Grouped Bar Chart

Search: Interactive grouped bar chart
- Compare outlets.
- Compare this month vs last month.
- Compare weekdays or months.
Data: Sales grouped by outlet and date.
3. Sales Activity – Calendar Heatmap

Search: Calendar heatmap analytics chart
- Identify high-revenue days.
- Discover weak business days.
- Show daily sales intensity.
Data: Sales transactions by date.
4. Peak Business Hours – Hourly Bar Chart

Search: Hourly sales distribution chart
- Breakfast, lunch and dinner performance.
- Identify peak business hours.
- Help plan kitchen operations.
Data: Sales transaction timestamps.
2. Food cost and wastage analytics
(Very high priority)

This is where Gulmohar can differentiate itself. You already have preparation, wastage, breakage, recipes and ingredient data.

5. Wastage Breakdown – Stacked Bar Chart

Search: Interactive stacked bar chart
- Compare wastage by item.
- Compare wastage by reason.
- Compare outlet-wise wastage.
- View quantity and cost.
Data: Wastage records, reasons and ingredients.
6. Preparation vs Wastage – Combo Chart

Search: Bar and line combination chart
- Prepared quantity vs wasted quantity.
- Wastage percentage as a line.
- Compare across days or items.
Data: Preparation logs and wastage logs.

This should become one of your signature charts.
7. Wastage Contribution – Pareto Chart

Search: 80 20 Pareto analysis chart
- Rank items by wastage cost.
- Show cumulative wastage percentage.
- Identify the few ingredients causing most losses.
Data: Wastage quantity, item cost and reason.
8. Food Cost Performance – Gauge Chart

Search: Interactive gauge chart with target
- Actual food cost percentage.
- Target food cost percentage.
- Performance against target.
Data: Ingredient consumption cost and sales revenue.

Requires reliable inventory/consumption and sales data before presenting an actual food cost KPI.
3. Menu and ingredient analytics
(High priority)

These charts can help owners decide which products and ingredients need attention.

9. Menu Engineering – Bubble Chart

Search: Menu engineering bubble scatter chart
- X-axis: Quantity sold.
- Y-axis: Profit margin per item.
- Bubble size: Total sales.
- Classify items as Stars, Puzzles, Plowhorses and Dogs.
Data: POS item sales + recipe cost.

Excellent executive-level analytics. Requires item-level sales and recipe costing.
10. Ingredient Cost Distribution – Treemap

Search: Interactive treemap chart
- Show cost contribution by ingredient.
- Larger blocks represent higher costs.
- Drill down into ingredient categories.
Data: Ingredient costs and consumption records.
11. Ingredient Consumption – Horizontal Bar Chart

Search: Ranked horizontal bar chart
- Top 10 consumed ingredients.
- Highest-cost ingredients.
- Compare outlets.
Data: Preparation/recipe consumption and ingredient quantities.
4. Outlet and operational analytics
(Multi-outlet management)

These are particularly important for the Manager (CEO) dashboard.

12. Outlet Performance – Radar Chart

Search: Multi series radar chart

Compare outlets using normalized measures such as:
- Sales performance.
- Wastage percentage.
- Preparation efficiency.
- Breakage cost.
Data: Outlet-wise operational and sales data.

Use normalized scores rather than mixing raw rupees and percentages.
13. Operational Performance – Heatmap

Search: Interactive matrix heatmap chart
- Rows: Ingredients or menu items.
- Columns: Outlets or dates.
- Cell intensity: Wastage percentage or cost.
Data: Wastage and preparation records.
14. Cost Impact – Waterfall Chart

Search: Interactive waterfall chart

Example:
- Revenue
- Ingredient cost
- Wastage cost
- Breakage cost
- Other expenses
- Remaining contribution
Data: Sales, costs, wastage and breakage.

Only show a true profit figure when all required cost components are available.

17. Wastage Flow – Sankey Diagram

Search: Interactive Sankey diagram

Visualize:

Ingredient → Preparation item → Wastage reason.

Useful to understand where losses originate. Requires reliable relationships between source items and preparation/wastage records.
18. Cost vs Wastage – Scatter Plot

Search: Interactive scatter plot chart
- X-axis: Ingredient cost.
- Y-axis: Wastage percentage.
- Identify expensive ingredients with high wastage.
Data: Ingredient cost and wastage logs.
19. Sales and Wastage Forecast – Forecasting Line Chart

Search: Time series forecast confidence interval chart
- Forecast sales.
- Estimate wastage trends.
- Show predicted ranges.
Data: Historical sales and wastage. Needs sufficient historical records and a forecasting model.
